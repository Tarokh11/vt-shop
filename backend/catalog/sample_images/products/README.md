# Stationery demo photos

These eleven representative demo photos were generated with the built-in
ImageGen tool. They are not photographs of actual branded inventory. Assets are
stored as WebP at quality 85; product slugs identify the corresponding files.

Prompt set: realistic e-commerce product photography, warm pale cream seamless
background, soft studio lighting, centered square composition, no branding,
legible text, logos, or watermarks. Individual subjects:

- `gel-pen-duo`: blue and black retractable gel pens, fine metal tips.
- `mechanical-pencil-05`: graphite gray and blue 0.5 mm mechanical pencils.
- `a5-lined-notebook`: sage A5 notebook, cloth cover and cream elastic band.
- `watercolor-pencil-set`: twelve watercolor pencils with color swatches.
- `desktop-organizer`: compact gray plastic organizer with stationery.
- `fountain-pen`: classic black fountain pen, polished nib, green accent.
- `pastel-highlighter-set`: six pastel highlighters in a fan arrangement.
- `dotted-notebook`: navy A5 dotted bullet journal with elastic closure.
- `a4-sketchbook`: open A4 sketchbook with colored pencil strokes.
- `metal-desk-tray`: dark green metal letter tray with blank sheets.
- `document-folder`: colorful cardboard folders and blank paper.

`seed_stationery` installs these photos for new products and repairs missing
media for existing sample products. Existing accessible uploads are preserved.
